What we solve

Five problems that hold back medical software startups.

To sell medical software in Europe you must show that it complies with Regulation (EU) 2017/745. This is how Amexa helps at every step.

01 · Qualification

“I don't know if my software is a medical device.”

It depends on what you promise, not on the technology. The same app can fall inside or outside the MDR because of one sentence in your instructions. And if your documents disagree, the notified body will read the strictest one.

How Amexa solves it

It reads all your documents, builds the product profile and applies the official MDCG 2019-11 decision tree, step by step.

  • Intended purpose, users, environment, AI and data, in one profile.
  • Flags when two documents contradict each other.
  • If it is not a medical device, you get the argument in writing.
Qualification · MDCG 2019-11

Example: LunarCheck

02 · Classification

“I don't know which risk class it has.”

The class decides the whole route: whether you need a notified body, what clinical evidence you will be asked for and how long it will take. Under the MDR, software tends to move up a class compared with the previous rules.

Software classification under Annex VIII typically results in software being up-classified, especially where it drives or influences the use of a medical device.
Source: PA Consulting, 2024
How Amexa solves it

It applies Rule 11 of Annex VIII and reasons the class step by step, with citations. If a lower class is defensible, it tells you exactly what you would have to change to get it.

Rule 11 · Annex VIII

Example: LunarCheck

IOther
IIaInforms decisions
IIbAlternative
IIIProposed
Why III: it provides information for diagnostic decisions where an error could cause death or irreversible deterioration. It could be IIb if every document limits the intended purpose to guiding the user, without diagnosing.
03 · Requirements

“23 requirements, dozens of sub-points and hundreds of pages to read.”

Annex I sets out 23 general safety and performance requirements, many of them with several sub-points. Filling in the matrix by hand, searching your documentation for evidence of each one, takes weeks.

How Amexa solves it

It splits the 23 requirements into 118 checks and reviews them one by one: whether it applies, what evidence your documents contain and what is missing. Every status rests on a verbatim quote.

  • Every quote is checked word for word against your document.
  • A quote that does not appear exactly is discarded and flagged.
  • No evidence, no “compliant”.

04 · Gaps

“I find out what is missing when it is too late.”

The notified body assessment is already long. If you arrive with an incomplete file, your application can be refused and the wait starts all over again.

Incomplete documentation at the time of application will lead to refusal of the application, which means the waiting time starts again.
Source: Matrix One, MDR guide for manufacturers
How Amexa solves it

It turns the matrix into a plan: what is missing, why it matters, which document closes it and how much effort it takes, ordered by priority. You see it on day one, not at the end.
